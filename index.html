AUMARA

REALMS

Aumara Realms is a biofeedback instrument for iPhone and iPad: nine living worlds of light and sound that mirror your inner state. Play them by touch, or wear a Muse S headband and let focus, calm, breath and heartbeat shape what you see and hear.

Support

Questions, problems, or ideas — write to eamonjbrown@gmail.com and you'll hear back from the person who made it.

Do I need a headband?

No. Choose Trace mode on the landing screen and every realm plays fully by touch. The Muse S headband is optional and connects directly to the app over Bluetooth — no other software needed.

Nothing is responding to my headband

Make sure the Muse is charged and worn snugly, with the band's sensors resting on your forehead and behind your ears. Realms connects to it automatically; give it a few seconds after the band is seated. If it drops out, the app keeps retrying on its own.

Sound

Every realm is played as much as it is watched. Headphones, a dark room, and no hurry.

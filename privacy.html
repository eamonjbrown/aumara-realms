Privacy Policy

Aumara Realms collects no data. The app has no accounts, no analytics, no advertising, and no networking code of any kind — nothing you do in it ever leaves your device.

If you connect an EEG headband, its sensor signals (EEG, heart rate, and breath) are processed on your device in real time to drive the visuals and sound, and are discarded as they are used. They are never stored, never transmitted, and never shared with anyone, including us.

Settings you adjust in the app are saved only on your device.
